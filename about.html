About us

Construction and stone work from Ayodhya.

Who we are

Shiva Enterprises is a construction business based in Ayodhya. We take up residential and commercial construction, along with red stone and marble work, in Ayodhya, Lucknow and nearby cities.

We have worked on the Ram Mandir construction, where our finished work includes mandap pavilions and boundary walls in stone.

How we work

You tell us what you want to build. We discuss the plan and the budget with you, then we build it and keep you updated until the work is complete.
